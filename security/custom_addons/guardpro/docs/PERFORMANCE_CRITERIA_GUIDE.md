# Performance Criteria Quick Reference

## Active Criteria (Total: 100%)

### 1. Punctuality (18%)
**Type**: Automatic Calculation  
**Description**: Measures on-time arrival for scheduled shifts with 5-minute grace period  
**Scoring**:
- On-time (≤5 min): 100%
- 1-15 min late: 80%
- 16+ min late: 50%

---

### 2. Tour Completion Rate (22%)
**Type**: Automatic Calculation  
**Description**: Percentage of assigned tours completed successfully with all checkpoints scanned  
**Scoring**:
- Based on: (Completed Tours / Assigned Tours) × 100

---

### 3. Incident Response Quality (18%)
**Type**: Automatic Calculation  
**Description**: Quality and timeliness of incident reports including documentation and photos  
**Scoring** (Base: 60, Max: 100):
- Description quality: up to 15 points
- Photo documentation: up to 10 points
- Response time: up to 15 points
- Categorization: 5 points
- Follow-up actions: 5 points

---

### 4. Client Satisfaction (15%)
**Type**: Automatic Calculation  
**Description**: Client feedback ratings and satisfaction scores from surveys and reviews  
**Scoring**:
- Based on: (Average Rating / 5) × 100
- Default: 80% if no feedback

---

### 5. Shift Adherence (15%)
**Type**: Automatic Calculation  
**Description**: Attendance and completion of scheduled shifts without unauthorized absences  
**Scoring**:
- Based on: (Attended Shifts / Scheduled Shifts) × 100

---

### 6. Communication Quality (7%)
**Type**: Manual Entry  
**Description**: Effectiveness of communication with team, supervisors, and clients  
**Evaluation Factors**:
- Clarity of communication
- Responsiveness to messages
- Professional tone
- Timely reporting

---

### 7. Professionalism (5%)
**Type**: Manual Entry  
**Description**: Professional conduct, appearance, and adherence to company standards  
**Evaluation Factors**:
- Uniform and appearance
- Conduct and behavior
- Company policy adherence
- Client interaction quality

---

## Badge Achievement Criteria

### Perfect Punctuality
- No late arrivals (>5 min) for 3 months
- Minimum 30 shifts required

### Perfect Attendance
- No missed shifts for 3 months
- Minimum 30 shifts required

### Tour Master
- 100% tour completion rate for 1 month
- Approved performance review required

### Client Favorite
- Average rating ≥4.8/5 for 3 months
- Minimum 5 feedback entries required

### Safety Champion
- No medium/high/critical incidents for 6 months
- Minimum 60 shifts required

### Top Performer
- 90+ performance score for 3 consecutive months
- Approved reviews required

### Service Milestones
- Automatic award at 1, 3, 5, and 10 years of service
- Based on first contract date

## Review Workflow

1. **Draft** → System creates review with period dates
2. **Calculate Metrics** → Automatic scores calculated
3. **In Progress** → Reviewer adds manual scores and comments
4. **Completed** → Review finalized by reviewer
5. **Approved** → Manager approves review
6. **Guard Acknowledges** → Guard signs off on review

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why are some criteria automatic and others manual?**  
A: Automatic criteria are based on measurable data (attendance, tours, incidents). Manual criteria require subjective evaluation (communication, professionalism).

**Q: Can criteria weights be adjusted?**  
A: Yes, but total must equal 100%. Contact system administrator to modify weights.

**Q: How often are badges awarded?**  
A: Badges are automatically checked and awarded monthly via scheduled job.

**Q: What happens if I disagree with my review?**  
A: Add your comments in the "Guard Comments" section before acknowledging. Discuss concerns with your supervisor.

**Q: Can I see my performance history?**  
A: Yes, all reviews are available in your profile under the Performance tab.

# Equipment Management

Complete guide to managing security equipment and assets in GuardPro.

---

## Overview

Equipment Management helps track security equipment, assign to guards, schedule maintenance, and manage inventory of uniforms, radios, flashlights, and other security gear.

---

## Equipment Types

**Common Equipment:**
- Radios/Walkie-talkies
- Flashlights
- Uniforms
- ID cards/badges
- Body cameras
- Weapons (for armed guards)
- Keys and access cards
- First aid kits
- Fire extinguishers
- Patrol vehicles

---

## Adding Equipment

### Navigation
- GuardPro > Site Management > Equipment
- Click **"Add Equipment"**

### Equipment Information

| Field | Mandatory | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Equipment Name** | ✅ Yes | Item description |
| **Equipment ID** | ✅ Yes | Unique identifier |
| **Category** | ✅ Yes | Type of equipment |
| **Site** | ✅ Yes | Location assigned |
| **Serial Number** | ❌ No | Manufacturer serial |
| **Purchase Date** | ❌ No | When acquired |
| **Warranty Expiry** | ❌ No | Warranty end date |
| **Condition** | ✅ Yes | New/Good/Fair/Poor |

---

## Equipment Assignment

### Assigning to Guards

**Step 1:** Open equipment record

**Step 2:** Click **"Assign to Guard"**

**Step 3:** Select guard and date

**Step 4:** Guard signs acknowledgment

**Step 5:** Issue recorded

### Tracking Assignments

**System tracks:**
- Who has what equipment
- When issued
- Expected return date
- Condition when issued
- Guard signature

---

## Maintenance Management

### Scheduling Maintenance

**For Each Item:**
- Maintenance frequency
- Last service date
- Next service due
- Service provider
- Maintenance notes

**Automatic Reminders:**
- 7 days before due
- On due date
- Overdue alerts

---

## Equipment Reports

**Available Reports:**
- Equipment inventory
- Assignments by guard
- Maintenance schedule
- Missing/lost equipment
- Replacement needs

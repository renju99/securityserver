# Attendance Tracking

Complete guide to tracking guard attendance and time management in GuardLink.

---

## Overview

Attendance Tracking allows you to monitor guard check-in/check-out times, track attendance rates, manage late arrivals, and handle no-shows.

---

## Guard Check-In Process

### Mobile App Check-In

**Step 1:** Guard opens GuardLink mobile app

**Step 2:** Taps **"Check In"** button

**Step 3:** System captures:
- ✅ Check-in time (auto)
- 📍 GPS location (auto)
- 📷 Photo (optional)
- 📝 Notes (optional)

**Step 4:** Confirmation displayed

### Desktop Check-In

**For office/reception based check-in:**

**Step 1:** GuardLink > Guards > Attendance

**Step 2:** Click **"Check In Guard"**

**Step 3:** Select guard from list

**Step 4:** Confirm check-in

## Handling Late Arrivals

### Late Check-In Process

**When guard checks in late:**

**Step 1:** System flags as "Late"

**Step 2:** Guard must add reason:
- Traffic delay
- Vehicle issue
- Personal emergency
- Other (specify)

**Step 3:** Supervisor receives notification

**Step 4:** Supervisor reviews and:
- Approve with note
- Request more information
- Mark for disciplinary review

---

## Managing No-Shows

### No-Show Detection

**System automatically detects when:**
- Guard doesn't check in within 15 minutes of shift start
- No communication received
- Location services show guard not at site

**Automatic Actions:**
1. 🔴 Alert sent to supervisor
2. 📧 Email to backup guard
3. 📱 SMS to guard (where are you?)
4. 📝 Incident created
5. 🔔 Client notification (if configured)

### No-Show Resolution

**Supervisor Actions:**

**Option 1: Guard Will Arrive**
- Mark as "Running Late"
- Update expected arrival
- Notify client

**Option 2: Guard Cannot Come**
- Mark as "No Show"
- Activate backup guard
- Fill shift immediately
- Schedule disciplinary review

- Late threshold: 5 minutes after

**Break Rules:**
- Break duration allowed
- Break approval required
- Paid vs unpaid breaks

**Overtime Rules:**
- When overtime applies
- Approval requirements
- Rate calculations

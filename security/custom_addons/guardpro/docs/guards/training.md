# Training & Certifications

Complete guide to managing guard training and certifications in GuardPro.

---

## Overview

Training & Certifications management helps you track guard training completion, manage certification renewals, schedule training sessions, and ensure compliance with security industry regulations.

---

## Managing Certifications

### Adding Certifications

**Navigation:**
- Open Guard Profile
- Click **"Certifications"** tab
- Click **"Add Certification"**

### Certification Details

| Field | Mandatory | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Certification Type** | ✅ Yes | Type of certification |
| **Certificate Number** | ✅ Yes | Official number |
| **Issue Date** | ✅ Yes | When issued |
| **Expiry Date** | ✅ Yes | When expires |
| **Issuing Authority** | ✅ Yes | Who issued it |
| **Document** | ✅ Yes | Scanned copy |

**Common Certifications:**
- Security Guard License
- First Aid & CPR
- AED Certification
- Fire Safety Training
- Weapons Permit (armed guards)
- Defensive Driving
- CCTV Operations
- Access Control Systems

## Certification Renewals

### Expiry Notifications

**System automatically alerts:**
- 🟡 **90 days before:** "Renewal due soon"
- 🟠 **30 days before:** "Urgent renewal required"
- 🔴 **On expiry:** "Certification expired"

**Automatic Actions:**
- Email to guard
- SMS reminder
- Supervisor notified
- Training scheduled (if internal)
- Guard restricted from applicable shifts

### Renewal Process

**Step 1:** Guard completes renewal training

**Step 2:** Update certification record:
- New issue date
- New expiry date
- New certificate number
- Upload new certificate

**Step 3:** System clears alerts

## Training Programs

### Scheduling Training Sessions

**Navigation:**
- GuardPro > Training > Training Sessions
- Click **"Schedule Session"**

### Training Session Setup

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Training Title** | Course name |
| **Training Type** | Initial/Refresher/Specialized |
| **Instructor** | Who conducts training |
| **Date & Time** | When scheduled |
| **Location** | Where held (or Online) |
| **Duration** | Hours required |
| **Max Participants** | Capacity limit |
| **Prerequisites** | Requirements to attend |

### Enrolling Guards

**Step 1:** Open training session

**Step 2:** Click **"Enroll Guards"**

**Step 3:** Select guards:
- Filter by certification needed
- Filter by expiry date
- Select individuals or groups

**Step 4:** Send invitations

## E-Learning Courses

### Online Training

**Features:**
- Video lessons
- Interactive quizzes
- Progress tracking
- Certificate generation
- Mobile accessible

### Assigning Courses

**Step 1:** GuardPro > E-Learning > Courses

**Step 2:** Select course

**Step 3:** Click **"Assign to Guards"**

**Step 4:** Choose:
- Individual guards
- All guards at site
- Guards needing specific certification
- New hires (onboarding)

### Tracking Completion

**Dashboard Shows:**
- Enrolled guards
- In progress
- Completed
- Failed/needs retake
- Average completion time
- Quiz scores

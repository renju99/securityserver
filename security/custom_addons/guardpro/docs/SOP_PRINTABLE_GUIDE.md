# SOP Printable PDF Guide

## Overview

The GuardLink system now includes **two professional printable PDF formats** for Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that guards can easily print, download, and share.

---

## 📄 **Two Print Formats Available**

### **1. Full SOP Report** - Professional Documentation
- **Purpose**: Complete, detailed documentation for training and reference
- **Best For**: 
  - Training sessions
  - Comprehensive review
  - Client documentation
  - Audit compliance
  - Management review

### **2. Compact Field Report** - Quick Reference
- **Purpose**: Condensed format optimized for field use
- **Best For**:
  - Guards on duty
  - Quick reference cards
  - Pocket-sized printing
  - Mobile field operations
  - Emergency quick-checks

---

## 🖨️ **How to Print SOPs**

### **Method 1: From SOP Form View**

1. **Navigate to SOP**
   - Go to: **GuardLink → Training & Knowledge → Knowledge & SOPs → Standard Operating Procedures**
   - Click on any SOP (e.g., "Main Gate Access Control Procedure")

2. **Print Options**
   - In the header, you'll see two print buttons:
     - **"Print SOP (Full)"** - Blue button with printer icon
     - **"Print Compact (Field)"** - Light blue button with PDF icon

3. **Generate PDF**
   - Click your preferred format
   - PDF will be generated automatically
   - Download or print directly from browser

### **Method 2: From List View (Bulk Printing)**

1. **Select Multiple SOPs**
   - Go to SOP List View
   - Select checkbox next to SOPs you want to print
   - Click "Print" in the action menu
   - Choose format: "Standard Operating Procedure" or "SOP - Compact (Field Reference)"

2. **Bulk Generation**
   - System will generate PDFs for all selected SOPs
   - Each SOP will be a separate page in the PDF

## 💡 **Usage Scenarios**

### **Scenario 1: New Guard Training**

1. **Print Full SOPs**
   - Print all mandatory SOPs in full format
   - Use for classroom training
   - Guards can take notes on printed copies
   - Keep in training manual

2. **Print Compact for Field**
   - After training, print compact versions
   - Laminate for durability
   - Keep in guard post
   - Carry in pocket for quick reference

### **Scenario 2: Emergency Procedures**

1. **Print SOP-EV-001 (Evacuation) - Compact**
   - Post near emergency exits
   - Laminate and hang at assembly points
   - Keep in emergency binder

2. **Print SOP-FE-001 (Fire Emergency) - Compact**
   - Post near fire extinguishers
   - Keep in fire safety box
   - Train guards with quick reference

### **Scenario 3: Client Audit**

1. **Print All Approved SOPs - Full Format**
   - Professional documentation
   - Include in audit package
   - Show SIRA compliance
   - Demonstrate approved status

### **Scenario 4: Guard Post Reference**

1. **Print Site-Specific SOPs - Compact**
   - SOP-AC-001 (Access Control) for gate guards
   - SOP-PT-001 (Patrol) for patrol guards
   - SOP-CC-001 (CCTV) for control room
   - Laminate and post at workstation

## 🎨 **Customization Tips**

### **For Managers:**

1. **Add Company Logo**
   - Reports use `web.external_layout` template
   - Your company logo will appear automatically if configured
   - Go to: **Settings → Companies → Edit Company → Header/Footer**

2. **Custom Letterhead**
   - Configure company letterhead in Settings
   - Will apply to all SOP reports

### **For Print Quality:**

1. **Best Printing Settings**
   - **Full Report**: A4 paper, portrait, high quality
   - **Compact Report**: A4 paper, portrait, standard quality
   - **Field Cards**: Print 2-up or 4-up per page to save paper

2. **Lamination Recommendations**
   - Use 3mil or 5mil laminating pouches
   - Trim edges for professional look
   - Punch hole for binder or lanyard

## 📊 **Report Specifications**

### **Full SOP Report**
- **Paper Size**: A4 (210mm × 297mm)
- **Font Sizes**: 9px-14px
- **Color**: Full color with branded headers
- **Pages**: Variable (depends on SOP complexity)
- **File Name**: `SOP-[CODE].pdf` (e.g., `SOP-AC-001.pdf`)

### **Compact Field Report**
- **Paper Size**: A4 (210mm × 297mm)
- **Font Sizes**: 8px-11px
- **Color**: Color-coded headers and badges
- **Pages**: Typically 1-2 pages
- **File Name**: `SOP-Compact-[CODE].pdf`

## 🆘 **Troubleshooting**

### **PDF Won't Generate**
- **Issue**: Clicking print button does nothing
- **Solution**: Check browser popup blocker, allow PDFs

### **Missing Content**
- **Issue**: Some steps or checklist items missing
- **Solution**: Refresh page and try again, contact admin

### **Poor Print Quality**
- **Issue**: Text is blurry or cut off
- **Solution**: Use browser print settings, select "Print backgrounds"

### **Can't Find Print Buttons**
- **Issue**: No print buttons in header
- **Solution**: Make sure you're viewing an individual SOP, not the list

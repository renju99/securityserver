# Compliance Audits & Inspections

Complete guide to conducting compliance audits in GuardLink.

---

## Overview

Compliance Audits help ensure security operations meet industry standards, client requirements, and regulatory obligations through systematic inspections and documentation.

---

## Creating an Audit

### Navigation
- GuardLink > Compliance > Audits
- Click **"Schedule Audit"**

### Audit Information

| Field | Mandatory | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Audit Type** | ✅ Yes | Internal/Client/Regulatory |
| **Site** | ✅ Yes | Location to audit |
| **Audit Date** | ✅ Yes | When scheduled |
| **Auditor** | ✅ Yes | Person conducting |
| **Audit Scope** | ✅ Yes | What to audit |
| **Checklist** | ✅ Yes | Audit template |

### Audit Types

**Internal Audit:**
- Regular quality checks
- Monthly/quarterly reviews
- Process compliance
- Best practice verification

**Client Audit:**
- Client-requested reviews
- Contract compliance
- Service quality assessment
- Performance verification

**Regulatory Audit:**
- Government inspections
- License renewals
- Safety compliance
- Legal requirements

---

## Conducting the Audit

### Audit Checklist

**Common Checklist Items:**

**Guard Documentation:**
- ✅ All certifications current
- ✅ Background checks on file
- ✅ Drug tests up to date
- ✅ Training records complete
- ✅ Uniforms and equipment proper

**Operational Compliance:**
- ✅ Shift logs maintained
- ✅ Incidents properly documented
- ✅ Checkpoints scanned on schedule
- ✅ Reports submitted timely
- ✅ Procedures followed

**Site Compliance:**
- ✅ Emergency procedures posted
- ✅ Contact information current
- ✅ Equipment functional
- ✅ Access control working
- ✅ Safety equipment present

**Record Keeping:**
- ✅ Visitor logs complete
- ✅ Key/access logs maintained
- ✅ Vehicle logs updated
- ✅ Lost & found documented

### Recording Findings

**For Each Item:**
- **Status:** Pass/Fail/N/A
- **Notes:** Observations
- **Evidence:** Photos/documents
- **Priority:** Low/Medium/High/Critical

**Finding Types:**
- ✅ **Compliant:** Meets standards
- ⚠️ **Minor Issue:** Needs attention
- 🔴 **Major Issue:** Immediate action required
- ❌ **Non-Compliant:** Serious violation

---

## Corrective Actions

### Creating Action Plans

**For Each Finding:**

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Issue** | What needs correction |
| **Root Cause** | Why it happened |
| **Corrective Action** | How to fix it |
| **Responsible Person** | Who will fix it |
| **Due Date** | When must be fixed |
| **Verification Method** | How to verify fixed |

**Priority Levels:**

**Critical (24 hours):**
- Safety violations
- License/certification issues
- Serious breaches

**High (7 days):**
- Client requirements
- Process violations
- Documentation gaps

**Medium (30 days):**
- Minor improvements
- Optimization opportunities

**Low (90 days):**
- Nice-to-have improvements
- Long-term projects

---

## Follow-Up Audits

### Verification Process

**Step 1:** Wait for due date

**Step 2:** Verify corrective actions:
- Review documentation
- Inspect physical changes
- Interview staff
- Test procedures

**Step 3:** Update audit record:
- Mark as "Resolved" or "Still Open"
- Add verification notes
- Attach evidence

**Step 4:** Close audit or escalate

### Generating Reports

**Report Contents:**
- Audit summary

**Report Uses:**
- Management review
- Client submission
- Regulatory filing
- Contract compliance
- Continuous improvement

---

## Audit Scoring

### Calculation

**Formula:**
```
Compliance Score = (Passed Items / Total Items) × 100
```

**Rating Scale:**
- **90-100%:** Excellent
- **80-89%:** Good
- **70-79%:** Acceptable
- **60-69%:** Needs Improvement
- **Below 60%:** Unsatisfactory

---

## Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Schedule regular audits
- Use standardized checklists
- Take photos as evidence
- Be thorough and objective
- Follow up on actions
- Trend analysis over time

❌ **Don't:**
- Skip scheduled audits
- Rush through checklist
- Ignore minor issues
- Forget to document
- Delay corrective actions
- Overlook repeat issues
